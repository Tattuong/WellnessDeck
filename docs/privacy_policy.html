Privacy Policy – WellnessDeck

Last updated: August 2026

WellnessDeck is an offline lifestyle dashboard. Hydration, meal notes, movement, sleep you type, mood, journal, look, and coins stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload your deck. WellnessDeck does not diagnose and does not connect to HealthKit, wearables, a microphone, or location.

Data stored on device

  • Today, Build, and Pattern Studio entries you type
  • Coins, owned shop items, and active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads
  • Optional reminder times you turn on

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs and Remove ads (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N251.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Permissions

  • Internet – Play billing and remote config
  • Notifications – optional local reminders (8:00, 13:00, 20:30). Requested only if you allow alerts. No tracking, no HealthKit.
