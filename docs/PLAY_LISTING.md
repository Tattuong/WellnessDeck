## Full description

WellnessDeck is your offline lifestyle dashboard. No diagnosis. Just insight.

Open Today to log hydration, meals, movement, sleep you type yourself, mood, a 4-7-8 breathing sit, and a daily intention. Nothing is uploaded. WellnessDeck does not connect to HealthKit, wearables, a microphone, or location.

Build the day with a workout checklist, meal notes, a habit stack, a screen-break timer, a self-care menu, a journal, and optional local reminders. Pattern Studio shows 7 or 30 days of charts, streaks, habit experiments, and a weekly reflection. Observations only — not medical advice.

Make it yours. Earn coins from the daily claim and lucky spin, or buy coin packs on Google Play. Unlock themes, backgrounds, card styles, and extras like Hydrate Plus, Meal Plus, Move Plus, Breath Plus, Break Plus, Care Plus, Pattern 90, and Journal Plus. Bought looks and features apply instantly; you can switch back to the default look anytime. Remove ads is a one-time Play purchase.

WellnessDeck is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Coin packs and Remove ads go through Google Play Billing.

Private. Offline. Yours.
